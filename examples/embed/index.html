Embed mode

Two Bonnard cells inside this page's own cards. Each is an iframe on #embed, sandboxed with allow-scripts only, fed a payload over bonnard:render. The chart fills its card; the second cell auto-sizes from the bonnard:size reports shown next to its header.

Requires a root pnpm build first (populates packages/widget/dist), then serve the repo root (python3 -m http.server 8000) and open /examples/embed/.
